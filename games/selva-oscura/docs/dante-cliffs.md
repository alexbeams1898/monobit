# Dante's *Divine Comedy* — Park Notes

The whole of the *Commedia* crushed into a sinner's pocket. For quick reference
when naming things, writing flavor copy, or settling arguments about what's
"on-theme."

---

## The frame

- **Written:** ~1308–1320 by Dante Alighieri, a Florentine exile.
- **Shape:** Three books — *Inferno*, *Purgatorio*, *Paradiso* — each 33 cantos
  (+ an opening canto in *Inferno*, total 100). Written in **terza rima** (linked
  triplets) in Italian, not Latin — a radical choice at the time.
- **The journey:** Dante (age 35, "midway in the road of our life") wakes lost
  in a dark wood, is rescued by the ghost of **Virgil** (Roman poet), and led
  down through Hell, up the mountain of Purgatory, and then — handed off to
  **Beatrice** (his idealized love) — through the heavens to God.
- **Point of it all:** a Christian allegory for the soul's correction. Sin →
  repentance → grace.

## INFERNO — 9 circles, sins of increasing weight

Hell is a funnel narrowing toward Satan at the earth's center. **Contrapasso:**
the punishment mirrors the sin.

| # | Circle | Sin | Punishment (the hook) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Limbo | Virtuous pagans / unbaptized | No torment, only sighs — they live without hope of God |
| 2 | Lust | Carnal sinners | Blown about forever by a black storm-wind |
| 3 | Gluttony | Gluttons | Lie in cold filthy rain, clawed by **Cerberus** |
| 4 | Greed | Hoarders & squanderers | Roll heavy weights against each other, forever |
| 5 | Wrath | Wrathful / sullen | Fight in the marsh of **Styx**; the sullen gurgle beneath it |
| 6 | Heresy | Heretics | Burn in open tombs |
| 7 | Violence | Against others / self / God | Three rings: a river of boiling blood; a wood of suicides turned to trees; a desert under rain of fire |
| 8 | Fraud (**Malebolge**) | Simple fraud | Ten *bolge* (ditches): panderers, flatterers in shit, simoniacs upside-down, sorcerers with heads reversed, corrupt politicians in boiling pitch, hypocrites in leaden cloaks, thieves bitten by serpents, false counselors as tongues of flame, schismatics hacked apart, alchemists/falsifiers diseased |
| 9 | Treachery | Betrayers | Frozen in the lake **Cocytus**; four rings (kin / country / guests / lords) |

At the very bottom: **Lucifer**, three-faced, frozen waist-deep, chewing on
Judas, Brutus, and Cassius (betrayers of Christ and Caesar).

**Key guardians:**
- **Charon** — ferryman of the dead across the Acheron
- **Minos** — judge of the damned; his tail winds to number the circle
- **Cerberus** — three-headed hound
- **Plutus** — guardian of greed
- **Phlegyas** — ferryman of the Styx
- **Medusa** / **Furies** — at the gates of Dis
- **Minotaur** — guards violence
- **Centaurs / Harpies** — patrol the blood-river and wood of suicides
- **Geryon** — beast of fraud (honest face, serpent body, scorpion tail)
- **Malebranche** — the ten "Evil-Claws," demons of the pitch
- **Giants** — ring the final pit (Nimrod, Ephialtes, Antaeus)

## PURGATORIO — 7 terraces for the 7 deadly sins

A mountain on the far side of the earth. Souls climb it to purge their sins
before entering Heaven. **Order matches the weight of the sin** — the worst
purged first (lowest terrace), the lightest last (nearest the top).

| Terrace | Sin | Penance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pride | Bent double under stones |
| 2 | Envy | Eyes sewn shut with wire |
| 3 | Wrath | Walk blind in acrid smoke |
| 4 | Sloth | Run endlessly |
| 5 | Greed | Lie face-down on earth |
| 6 | Gluttony | Starve near fruit they cannot eat |
| 7 | Lust | Walk through a wall of fire |

At the summit: the **Earthly Paradise** (the Garden of Eden). Virgil departs
here — as a pagan he can go no further. **Beatrice** arrives to take Dante up.

## PARADISO — 9 heavenly spheres + the Empyrean

Ascending through Ptolemaic cosmology: **Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars,
Jupiter, Saturn**, then the **fixed stars**, the **Primum Mobile**, and finally
the **Empyrean** — the abode of God, outside space and time. Each sphere holds
a different class of the blessed (inconstant souls, ambitious, lovers, wise,
warriors, just rulers, contemplatives, apostles…).

Dante sees the **Celestial Rose** — the seat of all the saved — and ends
with a direct vision of the Trinity: three rings of light, one of which
wears a human face. His will is finally aligned with "the Love that moves
the sun and the other stars."

## Cultural vocabulary worth stealing

- **Terza rima** — the linked-triplet verse form.
- **Contrapasso** — the punishment fits the sin (core Dante mechanic).
- **"Abandon all hope, ye who enter here"** — inscription over Hell's gate.
- **"In the midway of this our mortal life"** — opening line.
- **"The Love that moves the sun and the other stars"** — final line.
- **Dis** — the fortified lower city of Hell (circles 6–9).
- **Acheron / Styx / Phlegethon / Cocytus** — Hell's four rivers (sorrow,
  hate, boiling blood, frozen treachery).
- **Sangue** — Italian for *blood*; Phlegethon is the river of it.
- **Vita** — *life* — literally the poem's first image ("nel mezzo del
  cammin di **nostra vita**").
- **Ira, Furia, Avarizia, Superbia, Invidia, Accidia, Gola, Lussuria** —
  the seven deadly sins in Italian (wrath, fury, greed, pride, envy,
  sloth, gluttony, lust).

## What's useful for *Selva Oscura*

This document is **source-material reference only.** The active game
design lives in `docs/design/` (start with `setting.md` and `story.md`).
Use this doc to settle questions about what's *on-theme* vs. invented.

- The **9 circles** structure maps onto the descent (per setting.md
  *Geography*).
- **Contrapasso** — keeper behavior reflects the circle's sin. Per
  setting.md *Contrapasso - the law of form* and *Per-circle
  reactivity*, the breaking of contrapasso when a keeper falls
  (and the surfacing sangue that follows) is a real cosmological
  mechanic, not just thematic flavor.
- **Beatrice** — fully in scope. See `docs/design/setting.md` and
  `docs/design/story.md` for her role.
- **Satan at the bottom** chews Judas, Brutus, Cassius — used as the
  Hell's-wave boss in the post-Lucifer cosmological response (per
  setting.md *Cocytus and the post-Lucifer arena*).
- **Mary, the Angel of the Gate of Purgatory** — used as Purgatory's-
  wave and Heaven's-wave bosses respectively. See setting.md.
- **Charon's *non sei di qua* (Inf. III.88)** — the canonical
  cosmological refusal echoed in the death-card's *THOU DOST NOT
  BELONG* (per setting.md *Second death*).
- **Inferno I:117** (*"ch'a la seconda morte ciascun grida"*) — the
  thematic anchor for forced repentance via second death (setting.md
  *Forced repentance through second death*).
- **The selva oscura** — Inf. I, Dante's dark wood. In *Selva Oscura*,
  this is the game's hub *and* the cosmological destination of the
  unjudged class. Working title.
