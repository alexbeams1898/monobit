# Ars Goetia integration — parking lot

Captured 2026-06-05. NOT canon yet. Brainstorm notes to revisit when authoring named demons.

## The idea

Use the 72 demons of the Ars Goetia (part of the Lemegeton / Lesser
Key of Solomon, 17th c.) as the naming + iconographic foundation for
Selva's named individual demons. Every named demon gets its own
authored sigil, lifted from public-domain Goetic source material.

## Why it fits

Almost zero direct overlap between Dante and the Ars Goetia (Dante's
demons are classical: Charon, Cerberus, Minos; the Malebranche are
his own invention). But thick indirect lineage — both descend from
Christian-Augustinian demonology + scholastic taxonomy, both
structure evil as ranked bureaucracy. Cousins, not rivals.

The Ars Goetia is essentially a pre-built database of 72 named
individuals with: rank (King/Duke/Prince/etc.), function (Bael
teaches invisibility, Forneus teaches rhetoric, Marbas knows
mechanics), sigil (unique calligraphic/geometric seal), legion
count, direction. That's content goldmine for naming the keepers,
their lieutenants, and significant NPC demons without inventing
names from scratch.

Matches Selva's existing named-individual doctrine (Lonza/Leone/Lupa
are proper names of specific Named Things, not species labels —
[[project_selva_wood_lore_locked_2026_05_31]]).

## Scope discipline (locked direction)

**Named individuals only.** Keepers + their lieutenants + specific
dialog-bearing demons get Goetic names + sigils. Trash mobs (soul
larvae, ambient shades, swarm enemies) stay nameless. Sigils only
attach to entities that earn a Grimoire entry. Everything-named
loses signal.

## Three layers of use (ship in order)

1. **Grimoire-only (v1).** Sigil appears in the bestiary entry,
   never in the world. Pure collectible/identification layer.
   Easy. Doesn't touch combat or worldgen.
2. **In-world signage (v2, per-circle authoring).** Sigils etched
   into walls, floors, keeper-seats where the named demon
   manifests. Players who've read the Grimoire entry recognize what
   they're walking into before the demon shows up. Cosmologically:
   each ring's degradation includes its corrupt-keeper's mark
   spreading like graffiti.
3. **Mechanical invocation (v2+, if ever).** Drawing the sigil
   summons/binds/unbinds the named demon. This adopts the Solomonic
   operational frame and is significant scope. Defer.

## Mapping demons to circles (preliminary)

Function-based draft, not locked:

- **Asmoday / Asmodeus** → Lust (demon of lust in Solomonic +
  biblical Tobit tradition)
- **Belphegor** → Sloth (medieval association)
- **Mammon** → Greed (not Goetic strictly but same demonological
  tradition — out-of-bounds if "Goetic only" is the rule)
- **Beelzebub** → Gluttony (medieval association — also out of
  strict Goetic scope; he's higher than Goetic rank)
- **Belial** → Treachery / Cocytus (lawlessness, traitorous; King
  of Hell in some traditions)
- **Leviathan** → Envy or the swallowing-rivers iconography for
  Acheron
- **Limbo scholar-demons** (Forneus / Buer / Marbas) → these teach,
  don't torment, so they don't fit a punishment-keeper role. They
  fit Limbo's "virtuous pagan" register surprisingly well — but as
  the *demonic mirror* of the classical philosophers Dante puts
  there. Interesting layer: virtuous pagans on one side of Acheron,
  scholar-demons on the other, both teaching, both unable to leave.

Many Goetic demons don't map cleanly to sin categories. Use those
for mid-tier figures (Dukes/Marquises) based on individual
function, scattered across rings where they fit.

## Solomon-as-cosmological-figure question

Dante places Solomon in the Heaven of the Sun (Paradiso X-XIV) —
wisdom-circle, saved. If Selva honors that:
- (a) Solomon is in Heaven, his binding of demons was a divinely-
  sanctioned act, the Goetic grimoires are corrupted echoes of that
  legitimate work
- (b) Solomon's bindings were unauthorized, the bound demons are
  now LOOSE in Hell because the bindings degraded post-mortem,
  this is part of why each ring is chaotic — ties Solomon
  mythology into the Lucifer-act degradation timeline

(a) is cleaner. (b) is interesting but adds a second
cosmological-failure axis. Defer until we know which way the
Solomon thread wants to pull.

## Pre-corruption / post-corruption naming arc

Per [[project_lucifer_act_root_cosmology]] + setting.md "Hell's
failure": corrupt keepers are mangled originals. They had an
identity BEFORE the corruption. The Goetic name is the
post-corruption name.

Grimoire entry could surface this:
- **Goetic name (active threat)** — "BAEL, currently King of the
  East, manifests as three-headed (toad-cat-man), commands 66
  legions"
- **Sigil (current corrupted form)** — public-domain Bael sigil
- **Pre-corruption fragment** — what little is known of the
  original (often nothing concrete)
- **Post-restoration name** — after the Vagrant kills the keeper
  and Beatrice routes the role into the prepared NPC, the new
  mangled keeper takes a NEW name. Possibly a hybrid of the
  original divine name and the Goetic corruption, marking the
  restoration's incompleteness.

Visual: sigil could MUTATE on restoration — original Bael sigil
with cracks running through it, then redrawn as a wounded version
for the new keeper. Sigil-evolution as visual progression
indicator.

## Source material

Public-domain. Wikipedia "Ars Goetia" has the full demon list with
sigils. Higher-res scans available from various 19th-c. reproductions
(Mathers/Crowley edition is canonical). Look for clean line-art
versions for in-game use.
