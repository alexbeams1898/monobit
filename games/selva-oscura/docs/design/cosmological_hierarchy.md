# Cosmological hierarchy

Quick-access reference for "what kind of being is X." Pulls together
the categories scattered across setting.md / bestiary.md / classes.md /
creatures.md so the hierarchy is in one place.

**Status:** reference doc; not itself the doctrinal source. Each
category cites the source-of-truth lock. If this doc and the
sources disagree, the sources win — fix this doc.

## The hierarchy at a glance

```
FALLEN ANGELS (created by God as angels; rebelled with Lucifer; fell into Hell)
│  Pure-spirit beings whose natural assumed-form is human (the
│  rational-creature default). At the fall they were locked into
│  punishment-forms that distort away from human in proportion to
│  the severity of their rebellion. They fell at one event at the
│  founding of Hell and have been in their current forms since;
│  no "before" inside Hell, only a before in Heaven (pre-fall).
│  The apparatus of Hell IS the fallen-angel host doing its
│  assigned functions.
├── Lucifer (highest fallen; frozen at Cocytus's pit)
│   │  His was the greatest fall; his punishment-form is the most
│   │  distorted: three-faced, bat-winged, giant, frozen waist-deep
│   │  in ice, chewing the three arch-betrayers. Per setting.md
│   │  *Hell's failure*: the ROOT CAUSE of Hell's failure, absent
│   │  from the world the player encounters.
├── classical guardians (high-rank, keeper-seats)
│   │  Heavy distortion from the human default — their rank +
│   │  the symbolic requirements of their seats produced their
│   │  iconic shapes. Drawn from Dante by name.
│   │  At game-start, those still at their seats are corrupt residue
│   │  (further twisted by centuries of warped sangue absorption);
│   │  per setting.md *Hell's failure*.
│   ├── Cerberus       — three-headed dog, gluttony's gate (Inferno VI)
│   ├── Minotaur       — bull-headed, atop circle of violence (Inferno XII)
│   ├── Geryon         — fraud's mount (Inferno XVII)
│   └── (others per ring as authored)
└── lesser devils (lower-rank, enforcement)
    │  Minor distortion — recognizably humanoid (horns, hooves,
    │  vestigial tail, demonic skin tones). Their fall was less
    │  catastrophic than the classical guardians', so they retain
    │  more of the default human form.
    │  Some may have displaced into vacated keeper-seats as parasite
    │  replacements after the original guardians were destroyed.
    └── Malebranche (eighth circle, fifth pouch — Inferno XXI–XXIII)

SOULS (lived a mortal life; human-spirit-substance)
├── unjudged souls (carry no imprint; Hell never measured them)
│     │  natural destination = the selva oscura
│     │  killed by Hell's protocol but the protocol cannot COMPLETE
│     ├── pre-Signing Vagrant
│     ├── Guide
│     └── future arriving Wood NPCs (Beatrice-arranged, sparse)
│
└── judged / damned souls (Hell measured them; gave them an imprint
      │  of their sin's shape; live in the circle their sin lands
      │  them in; Hell's protocol CAN complete on them = second
      │  death ends them)
      │  Examples by circle (heavily abbreviated):
      │   Limbo:    Virgil, Aristotle, Plato, Homer (virtuous pagans
      │             + unbaptized — no active punishment, only
      │             suspended wistfulness)
      │   Lust:     Francesca da Rimini, Paolo Malatesta, Cleopatra
      │   Gluttony: Ciacco
      │   Greed:    anonymous corrupt clergy
      │   Wrath:    Filippo Argenti
      │   Heresy:   Farinata degli Uberti, Frederick II
      │   Violence: Pier delle Vigne (self-violence), Brunetto
      │             Latini (against nature), Capaneus (blasphemer)
      │   Fraud:    Ulysses, Guido da Montefeltro, Vanni Fucci,
      │             various corrupt popes
      │   Treachery: Ugolino della Gherardesca, Bocca degli Abati,
      │              Judas / Brutus / Cassius (chewed by Lucifer)
      │  In the failing-Hell state, most named historical figures
      │  may have degraded into anonymous pale shades over the
      │  centuries of corruption. Specific named appearances are
      │  per-encounter creative choices, not the default.
      └── post-Signing Vagrant (Hell-measured but unjudged-by-origin)

ANIMALS (Wood-only category; pre-failure native or sangue-leak descendants)
│  explicitly NOT Hell-creatures; NOT damned souls in animal-form;
│  NOT demons. Per creatures.md.
├── pre-failure native fauna  — mostly dead at game-start (Lonza, Leone fell;
│                                Lupa is the last surviving apex predator)
└── leak-evolved descendants  — new biology emerging from the per-keeper
                                sangue-leak as restoration progresses

EXTERNAL (origins outside Hell + the Wood; WIP)
└── Beatrice — agent acting on the Vagrant's run from outside the
              playable apparatus. Substrate-category deliberately
              left open; "External" is the holding label until her
              arc forces a more precise commitment.
```

## The Vagrant's specific position

This is the hierarchy question people ask most. The Vagrant moves
between two slots over the game, but **never leaves the SOULS
category** — he never becomes a fallen angel, never becomes a
classical guardian, never becomes a lesser devil.

**Pre-Signing (Beat 1–3):**
- Unjudged soul.
- No imprint. Hell never measured him.
- In the SOULS / unjudged sub-category.
- Same category as the Guide and any future arrivals.

**Post-Signing (Beat 4 onward, accept-path):**
- Still a soul. Still human-spirit-substance.
- The Signing is **Hell's first formal measurement** of him
  (per setting.md *The Signing installs the commit-fire*).
- He now carries an imprint Hell can grip — moves into the
  SOULS / judged-by-Hell sub-category structurally.
- Contrapasso can land on him from this moment.
- The Crucible's commit-fire installs substance into his substrate.
- **He does NOT become a fallen angel.** Fallen angels were never
  souls; they originated as pure-spirit beings created by God who
  rebelled. The Vagrant remains a soul, just a measured one.

**Post-Signing (Beat 4 onward, refuse-path = Unburdened):**
- Still a soul. Still unjudged.
- Stays imprint-free for the entire run; Hell never gets a grip.
- Beatrice's channel-fire routes held substance outward via
  *riversamento* instead of inward via the Crucible.
- The Unburdened is the only path that remains structurally
  unjudged from start to end.

## What the Signing actually does, in three bullets

Per [setting.md *The Signing installs the commit-fire*](setting.md):

1. Hell's first formal measurement of the soul.
2. The Guide installs the chrism-fire (the Crucible's metabolic
   fire that enables commit-into-substrate).
3. Contrapasso can land on the Vagrant from that moment.

The Signing does NOT:
- Make him a fallen angel.
- Make him an infernal being.
- Move him out of the soul category.

It moves him FROM the unjudged-soul slot TO the
measured-soul-with-installed-commit-fire slot. Both slots are
inside SOULS.

## What about the four classes (Penitent / Heretic / Wretched / Unburdened)?

Classes don't introduce a new being-category. They're **arrangements
of the same human-form substrate stats every soul carries** (per
[classes.md *Stat schema model*](classes.md)):

- The universal base — STR / DEX / END / LCK at 1/1/1/1 — is on
  every actor in the game: every player, every shade, every fallen
  angel, every NPC. It is the floor of being a measurable-form.
- For class-picker players (Penitent / Heretic / Wretched), the
  Crucible installs sangue into the substrate, growing the
  baseline values + later evolutions may add new unlocked
  stat fields (Heretic L2 unlocks Faith; Penitent L2 TBD;
  Wretched may add none and stay on the universal four).
- For the Unburdened, **the floor is the ceiling.** Stats stay at
  1/1/1/1 forever; no metabolic-fire means no installation.
  Unburdened evolutions unlock non-stat capability (passives,
  abilities, frames-of-immunity) instead.

So "Penitent" / "Heretic" / "Wretched" / "Unburdened" are not new
soul-types or new being-categories. They're paths the **same
human-form-soul Vagrant** takes after the Signing measures him.

## Why the cross-form combat-permission rule matters here

Per [bestiary.md *Soul-form vs animal-form combat doctrine*](bestiary.md):
- Soul-on-soul combat is forbidden (Roundtable peace).
- Soul-on-animal and animal-on-soul are permitted.
- Fallen angels (classical guardians + lesser devils + Lucifer)
  are infernal-creature category, NOT souls, so soul-on-them is
  permitted combat.

This is what makes the Lupa fight in the Wood permissible — she's
animal-form, the Vagrant is soul-form. It's also what makes future
classical-guardian boss fights structurally permissible:
Cerberus / Minotaur etc. are fallen-angel category, NOT souls, so
soul-on-them is permitted combat.

## Art / rig allocation — the form doctrine is also the art-budget rule

The same theological logic that distinguishes fallen-angel ranks
also distinguishes rig-reusable mobs from bespoke-mesh setpieces.
The two are the same insight at different layers:

- Pre-fall, fallen angels had no inherent form (pure spirit) — when
  they appeared, they appeared as humans (default rational-creature
  shape).
- At the fall, they were locked into punishment-forms that distorted
  away from human in proportion to the severity of their fall.
- This distortion-spectrum maps directly onto how much bespoke art
  spend each tier deserves.

**Rig-reusable population (the humanoid rig + mesh/texture
customization):**
- All damned souls in their circles — Limbo's pale shades, Lust's
  wind-blown, etc. The trash combat population.
- Lesser devils / Malebranche when they appear as enforcement mobs
  (horn/hoof/tail/skin-tint customization).
- The Vagrant, the Guide, any unjudged-soul NPC.
- Any restored/installed keeper who originated as a Beatrice-prepared
  NPC (per setting.md *Restoration*, the new keeper installs into a
  human-form soul who is then wounded into a keeper-shape; the
  underlying rig stays humanoid).

This covers the entire combat-mob population + most ongoing
world-content.

**Bespoke meshes (boss / setpiece spend):**
- Lupa (already done — her own rig).
- The classical-guardian keepers at the seats in their pre-restoration
  corrupt state: Cerberus, Minotaur, Geryon at minimum. Each gets a
  mesh expressing its canonical form distorted by centuries of
  corruption.
- Lucifer if/when he's fought — the most extreme setpiece (per
  setting.md, he is absent from the world the player encounters at
  game-start; whether he is ever fought is a story question).
- Possibly Beatrice in her post-saturation final-act forms
  (depends on how the endings express her transformation visually).
- Any leak-evolved Wood descendants that get apex-level treatment.

Manageable bespoke list — probably 8-12 unique fight-meshes across
the full game.

**The amortization that makes this work:**

Per setting.md *Restoration*, when the Vagrant kills the corrupt
keeper at a seat, the role re-installs into the Beatrice-prepared
NPC at that circle — and that NPC is a soul (human-form,
humanoid-rig-compatible). So even though the player fights a bespoke-mesh
corrupt keeper as a boss, the AFTER state (the restored keeper in
the post-victory world) can ride the standard humanoid rig with
appropriate wounded-keeper detailing.

Each keeper boss is fought **once** as a bespoke-mesh setpiece, and
after that the seat is occupied by a humanoid-rig wounded-NPC for
the rest of the game. Bespoke spend amortizes across one fight;
reusable rig handles all ongoing world-content.

**The principle:**

> Rig reuse for all combat-mob populations + restored keepers;
> bespoke meshes only for one-shot setpiece fights (corrupt keepers
> in their pre-restoration state, Lucifer, unique narrative
> encounters).

Cosmologically, the rig-reusable beings are those whose form is
closest to the human default — souls (which never left it) +
lesser devils (minor distortion from the fall). The bespoke meshes
are reserved for the extreme-distortion cases at the far end of
the fall-severity spectrum — the classical guardians and Lucifer.

The theology of fallen-angel form-distortion IS the economic logic
of when to spend bespoke art budget. They're the same insight.
