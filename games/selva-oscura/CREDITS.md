## Character art

### Humanoid base mesh (MB-Lab)

The player character mesh is generated by **MB-Lab** (a Blender add-on
for parametric humanoid character generation, fork of ManuelBastioniLAB).
The mesh is then rigged via Mixamo's auto-rigger service to bind it to
the standard Mixamo joint set so all Mixamo animation clips play
correctly.

- **Source:** https://github.com/animate1978/MB-Lab
- **Asset:** humanoid.fbx / humanoid.glb (under
  `assets/characters/humanoid/`)
- **License:** MB-Lab itself is licensed AGPL-3.0; the *generated
  character output* (mesh + textures) is released by MB-Lab under
  the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal license per the MB-Lab
  documentation, free for any use.

### Mixamo auto-rigger (skeleton)

The humanoid skeleton is produced by Mixamo's auto-rigger applied to
the MB-Lab-generated naked mesh. The rigger emits a standard Mixamo
joint set (mixamorig:* names) so every Mixamo animation clip works
against it without per-clip retargeting work.

- **Source:** https://www.mixamo.com/
- **License:** Mixamo Terms of Use -- royalty-free for non-commercial
  and commercial projects. See
  https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

## Animation clips

The locomotion and combat animation clips (idle, walking, running,
sword_and_shield_attack, jab, hook, falling_to_roll, second_death, hit
reactions, etc.) are sourced from Mixamo's free animation library,
retargeted to the humanoid skeleton and baked to `.ozz` runtime
archives via the ozz-animation toolchain.

- **Source:** https://www.mixamo.com/
- **Pipeline:** Mixamo FBX export -> Blender clean (rebookend.py) ->
  ozz `gltf2ozz` baking -> `assets/characters/humanoid/baked-archive/*.ozz`
- **License:** Mixamo Terms of Use (same as above).

### Heightmap

The colle and surrounding wood terrain are procedurally generated by
`games/selva-oscura/scripts/gen_terrain_heightmap.py`. No third-party
heightmap data is used.

### Gather material props (Kenney Nature Kit)

The world-pickup meshes for Wood gather materials (`bark_scrap` -- a
fallen log, `damp_earth` -- a small dirt mound, `pale_lichen` -- a
small mushroom standing in for fungal growth) are sourced from Kenney's
**Nature Kit** asset pack. The three .glb files live under
`assets/world/materials/<material>/<material>.glb`.

- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication; no
  attribution required, freely usable in commercial and non-commercial
  work).
- **Attribution offered as courtesy:** Created by Kenney.

## Source-text references

The design and writing of Selva Oscura draws on Dante Alighieri's
*Commedia* and *Vita Nuova*. Reference texts used in `docs/reference/`
are public-domain critical editions of the original Italian alongside
Henry Wadsworth Longfellow's 1867 English translation. No copyrighted
modern translations are vendored.

- *Commedia* (Italian) -- public domain
- *Commedia* (Longfellow translation, 1867) -- public domain
- *Vita Nuova* (referenced in lore docs) -- public domain
