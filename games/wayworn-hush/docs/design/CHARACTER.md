# Wayworn Hush — The Character (writer's bible)

> **Owns:** who the pilgrim is, so the writing stays consistent and true.
>
> **A writer's bible. NEVER surfaced.** The player learns none of this, ever — no
> backstory is exposed, no biography stated. It exists only to make his voice and
> his attention consistent across everything authored. If a fact here never
> affects how he sounds, thinks, or what he notices, it does not belong here.
>
> **Fiction drawn from life, not a portrait.** The character is invented, informed
> by real experience, never a transcription of a specific person. The subject
> stays general ([THEME.md](THEME.md)); anyone who carried this feeling should be
> able to walk into him.
>
> **Status:** first pass, built in conversation. Expand as the writing reveals him.

## Who he is at the start

Everything mechanical begins at the floor — Spirit, the tree, every stat at
minimum. That floor has a personality, and it is the starting character:

- **Deadpan, apathetic, unserious.** When nothing is wrong: "whatever." When
  something is wrong: "whatever." Dry, clever, EarthBound-register one-liners.
- **The apathy is armor.** "Whatever" is a defense — a way of not caring, because
  caring means you might not be enough. It is not emptiness; it is a shield. This
  is load-bearing for the arc (below).

## The gap (the unresolved thing)

- **He was more than his days.** Intelligent, charismatic, creative — a genuinely
  unique person. But his life just asked him to work; ordinary days have no slot
  for what he was, and he never made one — maybe couldn't see there was one to
  make.
- **The tragedy is ordinary, not dramatic.** No wasted destiny, no grand
  unfulfilled genius. Just a person who was more than his life let on, doing his
  days, until they stopped. More common and more devastating than a wasted genius:
  it is most people.
- **This gap is what keeps him.** He could not see his own worth. Coming to see it
  — his capability, and that he mattered — is the whole arc ([THEME.md](THEME.md)).

## How he sees (load-bearing — this IS the game)

The entire game is him observing a world, so how he attends is the most important
thing about him. Three traits, and each maps to a layer of the game:

- **He sees the absurdity in things** -> the humor. The deadpan, the one-liners,
  the mundane puncturing the profound. The *thought* layer ([VOICE.md](VOICE.md)).
- **He sees the beauty in things** -> the sincerity. Why the observations aren't
  cynical: he genuinely sees how the light works, how the shell is made. The
  *observation* layer.
- **He asked great questions** -> the engine. Observation is a question; the game
  is built of things to ask questions about. His best thoughts land on a *question*
  that reframes, never a conclusion. This is why growth makes him more *himself*,
  not wiser.

**The questions are the quest system** ([STORY-FRAME.md](STORY-FRAME.md) §5). The
man who asked great questions becomes the literal mechanic by which the world
becomes legible. The structure of the game is the shape of his mind — and his
questions had nowhere to go in life; now they do.

## The arc, in his voice

Growth does not change what he sounds like — it changes what the "whatever" is
made of:

- **Early "whatever" = I refuse to care.** The deadpan is defensive; the apathy
  keeps the world at arm's length so it can't find him lacking.
- **Late "whatever" = I am at peace.** Same word, same dry delivery — but the
  armor is gone. He is genuinely unbothered because he is finally at ease with
  himself, not because he is hiding.

The humor never leaves and never softens into wisdom; it gets *funnier*, because
relaxed deadpan beats defensive deadpan ([VOICE.md](VOICE.md) §3). The player
measures his peace by what the apathy is protecting — and by the end, nothing.

## Life before (kept minimal — only what leaks into voice)

- He worked. Days passed. Nothing announced itself as significant. That is the
  whole shape, and it is the point — the gap between what he was and what his days
  asked of him. Further biography is not authored here: it would never reach the
  player and would only rot.

## Open (deliberately)

- Whether he *knew* he was more than his days and sat on it, or genuinely couldn't
  see it. Two different sadnesses; not yet chosen.
- The exact texture of the deadpan reaction per situation — left to emerge in the
  writing rather than pinned.
